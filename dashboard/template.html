How each metric is calculated
Cover % (ATS)
Covers ÷ (covers + non-covers). Pushes are left out of the percentage and shown separately in the record (W-L-P). 52.4% is roughly the break-even rate at standard −110 odds.
Over %
Overs ÷ (overs + unders). Pushes (final total exactly equal to the line) are left out and shown in the record.
Favorite / Underdog
The side favored by the closing point spread. If the spread is 0 (pick'em), the team with the shorter moneyline is the favorite.
Spread & totals
One consensus closing line per game from the nflverse dataset. No opening lines are available.
Margin of victory
Absolute point difference between the two teams. Ties count as 0.
Home-field advantage
Average home score minus away score. Neutral-site games (international games, Super Bowls) are excluded from all home/away stats.
Indoor / Outdoor
Indoor = dome or retractable roof closed. Outdoor = open-air stadium or retractable roof open.
Prime time
Kickoff at 7:00 PM ET or later (Thursday, Sunday and Monday night games, plus late Saturday/holiday games).
Divisional game
Both teams are in the same division (current 8-division alignment).
Team / Division filters
Keep only games involving that team (or any team in that division). On the Team ATS tab they instead choose which teams appear and highlight the selected team.

How to read this

  • 52.4% is the break-even win rate at standard −110 odds. A side has to win more often than that to make money. Rates are tagged above break-even, below 47.6% (the other side is above break-even) or coin flip.
  • Sample size matters. Anything tagged small sample (under 30 graded games) can easily be luck. A 7-3 record is not a trend.
  • The line already knows. Sportsbooks set lines using this same history, which is why most rates sit near 50%. Past results describe what happened; they do not predict the next game.
